HR POLICY MANUAL – CODE OF CONDUCT POLICY
I. PURPOSE
This policy establishes behavioral expectations for employees to maintain professionalism, integrity, and respect within the workplace.

II. SCOPE
Applies to all employees, contractors, and consultants.

III. CODE OF CONDUCT
Professional Behavior – Employees must maintain respectful communication and avoid harassment, discrimination, or offensive language.

Confidentiality – Employees must not disclose company or client confidential information without prior authorization.

Conflict of Interest – Employees must disclose any personal or financial interests that could interfere with their job responsibilities.

Company Assets – Misuse of company property, resources, or funds is strictly prohibited.

Compliance – Employees must comply with all applicable laws, company rules, and safety standards.

HR POLICY MANUAL – REMOTE WORK POLICY
I. PURPOSE
To provide guidelines for employees working remotely to ensure productivity, accountability, and data security.

II. SCOPE
Applies to employees approved for remote or hybrid work arrangements.

III. REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES
Eligibility – Employees may request remote work subject to manager and HR approval.

Working Hours – Remote employees must adhere to their assigned working hours unless otherwise approved.

Productivity Tracking – Employees must be available via email, chat, or video during work hours.

Data Security – Employees must use company-approved devices, VPN, and secure passwords.

Equipment – The company may provide laptops, monitors, and other tools for remote work.

HR POLICY MANUAL – PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT POLICY
I. PURPOSE
To establish a fair and transparent framework for evaluating employee performance and supporting career growth.

II. SCOPE
Covers all permanent employees.

III. PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
Appraisal Cycle – Performance reviews will be conducted annually, with mid-year check-ins.

KPIs & Goals – Employees will have measurable performance indicators set by managers.

Feedback – Managers must provide continuous feedback, not limited to review cycles.

Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) – Employees not meeting expectations will be placed on a PIP with clear milestones.

Rewards – High performers may receive bonuses, promotions, or recognition awards.

HR POLICY MANUAL – LEAVE EXTENSIONS & SPECIAL PROVISIONS
I. Half-Day Leave
Eligibility – All regular full-time employees are eligible for half-day leave.

Duration – Employees may avail up to 2 half-day leaves per month, counted as 0.5 day per instance.

Application – Must be applied through the Employee Self Service portal at least 2 hours before the intended leave.

Encashment & Carryover – Half-day leaves follow the same rules as the corresponding leave type (Vacation, Sick, etc.).

II. Emergency Leave
Eligibility – All employees are eligible for 2 days of emergency leave per year for unforeseen personal emergencies (accidents, natural calamities, urgent family situations).

Application – To be reported to the supervisor immediately by phone/email, followed by ESS portal submission.

Carryover – Emergency Leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

Encashment – Emergency Leave cannot be encashed.

III. Festival Leave
Eligibility – All employees are eligible for 2 Festival Leave days per year to celebrate religious or cultural events of their choice.

Application – At least 7 days in advance, subject to manager approval.

Carryover – Cannot be carried over.

Encashment – Cannot be encashed.

IV. Wellness Leave (Mental Health Days)
Eligibility – All regular employees are eligible for 2 Wellness Leave days per year.

Purpose – To support employees’ mental health and well-being.

Application – Can be applied without disclosing medical details, subject to approval.

Carryover & Encashment – Not permitted.

V. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Eligibility – Employees who have exhausted all leave balances may apply for LWP.

Duration – Subject to HR and supervisor approval, depending on circumstances.

Salary Impact – Pro-rata salary deduction will apply.

Probation – Employees on probation may also apply for LWP.
